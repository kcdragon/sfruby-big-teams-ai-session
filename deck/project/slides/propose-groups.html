LEVEL 2 · BIRDS OF A FEATHER

PROPOSE A TOPIC

Got something you want to talk about? Come up and pitch it. Then we break into groups around the topics.

About 5 to 7 minutes. Anyone can come up and propose a topic in a sentence or two. Seed it by having one or two people ready to go first, and repeat each topic into the mic so the back of the room hears it. Aim for 4 to 8 topics; merge any that overlap. Give each topic a spot in the room, then send everyone to the group they want.
