LEVEL 2 · BIRDS OF A FEATHER

PROPOSE A GROUP

1

Come to the mic

Anyone can propose a group. No slides, no prep.

2

Pitch it in one line

The topic, and who should join you.

3

Claim a spot

Point to a corner of the room. That’s your group.

Need an idea? Pick a boss: review, ownership, shared config, new builders, governance or the monolith.

About 5 to 7 minutes. Ask for proposals and keep each pitch to one sentence: the topic and who it's for. Seed it by having one or two people ready to go first. Send each proposer to a distinct spot in the room as they finish, and repeat each topic into the mic so the back of the room hears it. Aim for 4 to 8 groups. If two pitches overlap, merge them on the spot. Then send everyone to the group they want.
