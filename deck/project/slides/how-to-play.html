LEVEL 3 · BOSS LEVEL

TWO WINS & A REGRET

1

Write

Three decisions your team made about agentic coding.

2

Hide

Two worked. One backfired. Don’t say which.

3

Guess

Read all three. Your group guesses the regret.

4

Reveal

Say why it backfired and what you’d do instead.

About 15 minutes in groups, roughly 2 to 3 minutes per person. This is the session's two-truths-and-a-lie, adapted to Amanda's "living with decisions" theme. The regret is the real payload; the guessing just makes it fun. Have each group pick its best regret to share.
