RULES OF THE GAME

3 RULES FOR AN HONEST ROOM

1

Share the lesson, not the source

What’s said here leaves without names or companies attached.

2

Every builder counts

Engineers, founders, and people who ship code without the title.

3

Specifics beat hype

A real decision and how it turned out beats a hot take.

About 1 minute. Rule 1 is the Chatham House rule, and it matters for big-company folks, who often can't speak on the record. Rule 2 is for the vibe coders in the room: say explicitly that they're welcome. Rule 3 keeps the conversation grounded in real decisions rather than predictions.
