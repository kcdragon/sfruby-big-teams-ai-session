LEVEL 3 · PLAYER 2 PRESS START

YOUR TURN

Come up to the mic. Three decisions, two wins and one regret. The room guesses which one backfired.

The rest of the level, until 2:45. Leave this up while people take turns. Ask the next couple of players to wait by the stage so there is no dead air between turns, and seed the first one or two before the session. 2 to 3 minutes each: three decisions, a show of hands, then the reveal of what backfired and what they would do instead. At the end, the room can vote for the best regret by applause; a small prize like a Ruby sticker helps.
