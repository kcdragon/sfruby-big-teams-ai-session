LEVEL 1 · BONUS STAGE

OPEN MIC:
1 STRATEGY · 60 SEC

▶ A decision you’d make again

▶ Something you tried and killed

▶ A rule your team actually follows

About 12 minutes. Line up at the podium mic. Before the session, line up 2 or 3 people to go first so it doesn't start cold. Keep a visible 60-second timer on your phone. After each one, write the strategy on a sticky and put it on the wall. At the end, tease Level 2 for anyone about to leave for a talk.
