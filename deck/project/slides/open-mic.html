QUESTION 1 · CODE REVIEW

Most of our PRs are written by agents, but a human still reviews every one. Review is now the bottleneck. What are you doing about it?

About 12 minutes. You go first: explain that most PRs at your company are agent-generated, every PR still needs a human review, and review is now the bottleneck. Ask what people are doing about it, then let the room answer. Nudge toward specifics: smaller PRs, AI first-pass review, risk-based review, who is allowed to skip it. After a few minutes, invite the next person up to ask their own question. Line up 2 or 3 people beforehand so the second question doesn't start cold. At the end, tease Level 2 for anyone about to leave for a talk.
