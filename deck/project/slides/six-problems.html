LEVEL 1 · THE MAP

SIX BOSSES AT SCALE

1 · REVIEW

PR volume is up and reviewer count isn’t. Who reviews agent code?

2 · OWNERSHIP

Who’s on call for code nobody typed?

3 · SHARED CONFIG

Who owns the CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and rules that 50 teams use?

4 · NEW BUILDERS

PMs and designers are opening PRs. Guardrail or superpower?

5 · GOVERNANCE

Approved tools, data, cost and audit. Who gets to say yes?

6 · THE MONOLITH

Do Rails conventions make a big app agent-friendly or agent-hostile?

About 3 minutes. This is your framing, drawn from your own experience. Pick one or two of these and tell a short story from your own teams: [your story]. Don't lecture through all six. In Level 2 people propose their own groups, so these six are a menu of ideas for anyone who wants to pitch one. Another topic if the room asks: the junior pipeline, meaning how people learn a codebase that agents mostly write.
